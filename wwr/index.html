We run rivers.
We protect them too.

Founded by a group of passionate paddlers who wanted to share the rivers they loved, Rapids & Rivers has grown into a full-service outfitter — but our mission hasn't changed. Every booking sends a portion back to the river stewardship programs that keep these waterways wild.

Our guides are certified, our gear is top-of-the-line, and our safety record is spotless. We believe the outdoors should be joyful, accessible, and responsibly managed — for this season and every season after.
