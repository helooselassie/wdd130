Featured Adventures

Class IV–V Full Day

Los Dientes del Diablo

The Devil's Teeth — our most legendary run. Fourteen kilometres of back-to-back Class IV and V rapids through a sheer granite canyon that blocks the sun by noon. This trip is built for experienced paddlers who want the real thing: cold water, big drops, and an adrenaline rush that stays with you for years.

  • 14 km of continuous white water
  • Expert certified guide (1:4 ratio)
  • Riverside barbecue lunch included
  • Helmet, wetsuit & PFD provided
From $149 per person
Class III Half Day

Big Mallard Canyon

A classic Intermountain run through canyon walls painted amber and rust by centuries of river carving. Big Mallard mixes lively Class III rapids with long, glass-calm stretches perfect for spotting ospreys and river otters. Great for adventurous beginners or families with older children seeking a genuine white-water introduction.

  • Stunning 300 ft canyon walls
  • Wildlife spotting opportunities
  • Post-trip riverside snack stop
  • All safety gear included
From $79 per person
Class I–II Half Day

Half Day Family Float

No experience? No problem. Our Half Day Float was designed from the ground up for first-timers and families with young children. You'll drift through scenic meadow bends, splash through a handful of playful riffles, and learn the basics of team paddling — all in a relaxed, no-pressure environment led by our friendliest guides.

  • Suitable for ages 5 and up
  • Calm Class I–II water only
  • Guided paddling instruction
  • Photos included with booking
From $49 per person
Class II–III Evening

Sunset Canyon Run

Our most romantic offering — a 3-hour late-afternoon float timed so you hit the final canyon bend just as the sun drops behind the ridge and turns the water to copper. Includes a gourmet riverside picnic with local cheeses, charcuterie, and sparkling water. Ideal for couples, celebrations, or anyone who just needs the world to slow down.

  • Timed for the golden hour
  • Gourmet riverside picnic
  • Private booking option available
  • All gear provided
From $95 per person
Class V Full Day

Thunder Falls Expedition

Thunder Falls is the crown jewel of our lineup — a full-day expedition culminating in a roaring 12-foot drop that has appeared in two national outdoor magazines. Only guests with prior Class IV experience may join, and group sizes are capped at eight for maximum safety and immersion. This is the trip people train for.

  • 12-foot signature waterfall drop
  • Max 8 guests per departure
  • Shuttle, lunch & dry bags included
  • Prior Class IV experience required
From $189 per person
